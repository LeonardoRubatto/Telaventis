Founder — design & development

You work directly with the person who defines, designs and builds your website: Leonardo Rubatto, based in France.

A student in a Licence in Organisation Sciences at Université Paris-Dauphine (PSL), I picked up AI as one tool among others — and in an economy that gets more digital every year, knowing how to use it well felt like the obvious thing to do. Telaventis grew out of that interest: a student's time and availability, put to work on real projects, from the first message to launch.

Sites are written by hand, then deployed and archived with every change. That's a choice — speed, robustness, independence from a vendor — not a limitation to hide. The technical detail sits right alongside, for anyone who wants to read it.
