wecalc.fr

French calculators, with no ads, no sign-up, no page reloads.

Screenshot of the homepage, as it is live.

Context

Online calculators are saturated with ads and trackers: you expect a number, you get a slow, tracked page.

Design decisions

A tool that calculates as you type, no reload, no account to create, no third-party script. The result appears while you're typing — that's the whole demonstration.

What was delivered

Seven calculators with no ads and no sign-up, with an immediate result as you type.
