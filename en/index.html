Common
questions

Why a static site?

Because it's faster to serve, more durable over time, and free of vendor lock-in. No database and no plugin to update: the site still works exactly the same years from now.

Who works on my site?

You work directly with the person who defines, designs and builds your website, from the first message to launch.

I already have a site — do we start from scratch?

No. The "Rethink what exists" offer scales with what's already there: content that works is kept, the rest is rewritten — the price follows the actual amount of work, see the pricing page.

Which language can we work in?

French, English or Italian — for our exchanges as well as for the site's content.

How do we get started?

Through the contact form: your business, what the site needs to do, and a rough budget. Written reply within one business day.
